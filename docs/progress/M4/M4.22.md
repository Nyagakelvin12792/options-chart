# M4.22 Equidistant Tie-Breaking Validation

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Verification of deterministic lower-strike selection when multiple Gamma Flip zero crossings are equidistant from current spot.

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- When two qualifying zero crossings are equidistant from spot price ($|\text{price}_1 - S| = |\text{price}_2 - S|$), the headline selection rule deterministically chooses the lower strike price.
- Selection is order-invariant regardless of input crossing array ordering.
