# M4.15 Extreme Volatility Regimes

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Validation of engine stability under extreme low and high implied volatility regimes ($\sigma \in [0.01, 10.0]$).

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Extremely low volatility ($\sigma = 1\% = 0.01$) produces positive finite $d_1, d_2$ and gamma spikes centered at ATM.
- Extremely high volatility ($\sigma = 1000\% = 10.0$) flattens the distribution and maintains numerical stability without overflow across the spot grid.
