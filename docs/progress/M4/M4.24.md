## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Verification of aggregate holder payoff minimization and deterministic lower-strike selection on exact payoff ties.

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Holder payoff at evaluated spot $S$ is $\sum \max(0, S - K) \cdot \text{OI}_{\text{call}} + \sum \max(0, K - S) \cdot \text{OI}_{\text{put}}$.
- Max Pain is the candidate strike that minimizes aggregate payoff.
- On exact payout ties between multiple strikes, the lower strike price is selected deterministically.
