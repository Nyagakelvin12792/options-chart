# M4.18 Zero Open Interest Options

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Verification of options with zero open interest ($\text{OI} = 0$).

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Options with $\text{OI} = 0$ are valid listed contracts; they calculate gamma per dollar normally, contributing exactly $\$0.00$ to modeled dollar GEX and gross GEX.
- When total call or put OI is zero, the Put/Call ratio safely resolves to `0` without division by zero or NaN.
