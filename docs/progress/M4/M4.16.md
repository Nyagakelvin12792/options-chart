# M4.16 Missing IV Exclusions

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Validation of missing implied volatility handling (`markIvDecimal = null`).

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Contracts with `markIvDecimal === null` or `markIvDecimal <= 0` are excluded from continuous Black-Scholes gamma profiles and tracked in `excludedCountByReason.missingIv` / `excludedCountByReason.invalidIv`.
- Open interest and contract accounting retain missing IV contracts for total portfolio exposure tracking.
