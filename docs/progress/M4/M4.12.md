# M4.12 Near-Expiry Edge Cases

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Validate numerical stability and 15-minute floor exclusions for near-expiry contracts ($T < 1\text{h}$, $T < 15\text{m}$).

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Contracts with remaining time to expiry $< 15\text{ minutes}$ (`minimumProfileTimeToExpiryMs = 900,000 ms`) are excluded from continuous Gamma and GEX profiles to avoid $1/\sqrt{T}$ asymptotic singularities.
- Near-expiry contracts are preserved with 100% fidelity in Total Open Interest, Call/Put breakdowns, and Max Pain settlement calculations.
- Exclusions are explicitly classified under `nearExpiryProfileFloor` in calculation audit metadata.
