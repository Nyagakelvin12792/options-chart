# M4.13 0DTE and Expired Contract Handling

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Verification of 0DTE and post-expiry contract handling ($T \le 0$).

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Expired contracts where `expirationTimestamp <= calculationTimestamp` are partitioned into `excludedCountByReason.expired` and yield `calculateTimeToExpiryYears = null`.
- 0DTE options expiring later in the current session ($T > 15\text{m}$) are priced accurately and participate in intraday gamma profiles.
