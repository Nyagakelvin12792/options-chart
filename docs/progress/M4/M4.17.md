# M4.17 IV Normalization Verification

## Status: COMPLETE

- Owner: Antigravity
- Updated: 2026-08-26
- Scope: Verification of percentage-to-decimal implied volatility normalization.

---

## 1. Requirements & Specifications

- Deribit publishes IV in percentage points (e.g., `80.0` for 80% IV).
- Normalizers strictly convert this to decimal notation (`0.8000`) before mathematical evaluation.
- Prevents 100x variance suppression that would occur if percentage strings were passed directly to Black-Scholes formulas.
